drewops@proton.me

Quicker answers

  • What the app does with your data (nothing it shouldn't): the privacy policy.
  • Food lookups: turn either database off under Settings › Food Data Sources; the app then looks up foods only in its on-device catalog.
  • Your data: export it as CSV or JSON, or delete everything, under Settings › Data. Deleting the app removes it from that device only; data in iCloud stays until you delete it in the app or in your iCloud settings.
  • Apple Health: turn it on or off in the Health app.

When you write

  • The app version, shown in Settings › About in the app.
  • Your device and iOS version, and what you tried and saw.
  • Screenshots help a lot, if it looks wrong.
