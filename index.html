Diet App

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

The short version

Diet App doesn't collect your data. Your nutrition log, targets and body measurements stay on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app contains no third-party code that collects data.

What the app stores

The app stores your food log, calorie and macro targets, saved foods, meals and recipes, body measurements and settings. Everything is kept locally on your device. The developer has no access to it. You can delete your data at any time in the app under Settings › Data, or by deleting the app.

Food database lookups

When you search the online food database, scan a product barcode, or log a meal by description, the food text or barcode you entered is sent over the network to look up nutritional information. Barcode scans query Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org) first and fall back to the USDA FoodData Central database (api.nal.usda.gov); text searches and meal descriptions use USDA FoodData Central. These requests contain only the food names or barcodes you ask about — not your food log, body measurements or any other data in the app. You can turn each database off under Settings › Food Data Sources; the app then looks up foods only in its on-device catalog. Each provider's own privacy policy applies to its service.

Apple Health

Only if you turn it on, the app can read body weight, body fat percentage and height from Apple Health, and write dietary energy, protein, carbohydrates and fat to Apple Health. Health data is only used to power the app's nutrition tracking, is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone; it isn't sent to the developer. You can turn this off at any time in the Health app.

Apple Intelligence

Describing a meal in natural language uses Apple Intelligence on your device. Your food descriptions are processed on the device and aren't sent to the developer or to third parties. When the on-device model is unavailable, the app falls back to basic text parsing, also on the device.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to scan product barcodes and, when you choose to, to photograph a meal. You can also pick a meal photo from your photo library; the app only sees the photo you pick. Barcode images are processed on the device to read the barcode. Meal photos are analysed on the device by Apple Intelligence to suggest foods and portions, which you can edit before anything is saved. Images are never stored by the app and are never sent to the developer or to third parties.

Exports

CSV and JSON files are created on your device only when you choose to export. Where you send them is up to you, and the app or service you share them with handles them under its own privacy policy.

Children

Diet App isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page, with a new effective date.
